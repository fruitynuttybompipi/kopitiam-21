# Kopitiam 21 · 8 kopitiam games

A pixel-art multiplayer game set in a Malaysian kopitiam, with eight games: **Uncle Jack 21** (blackjack), **Telur Pong**, **Kopi Tumpah**, **Ingat Order**, **Cari Telur**, **Tarik Teh**, **Kira Duit** and **Lalat Hunt**. You play with friends in the browser, and each person uses their own phone or laptop. No sign-up and no server to run.

## Put it on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new **public** repo, e.g. `kopitiam-21`.
2. Upload **everything in this folder** and keep the folders as they are (`css/`, `js/`, `fonts/`, `index.html`, `.nojekyll`).
3. In the repo, go to **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment**. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, then branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, and save.
4. After a minute the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/kopitiam-21/`.

## Play with friends

1. Open the link, type your name, then pick an avatar and a drink.
2. One person taps **BUKA MEJA** (create table). They become the host and get a 4-letter code.
3. The host taps **MEJA ···· SHARE** to send the link. Friends can also type the code and tap **MASUK**.
4. The host can pick the **Tauke** (dealer) in the **TAUKE** menu. If nobody is picked, the Tauke is chosen at random. Then tap **START!**
5. You can have up to 6 people at a table: 1 Tauke and 5 players. The host can also add bots to fill seats.

## Telur Pong (2 players)

Pick **TELUR PONG** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA.

- Throw eggs into the other player's 6 kopi cups. **Swipe up** on the table (or drag up with the mouse): a longer swipe throws further, and tilting the swipe left or right aims.
- The ceiling fan blows the egg sideways. Check **ANGIN** before you throw; it changes every turn.
- 2 throws per turn. If both go in, it's **Telur balik!** and you throw 2 more.
- The first to sink all 6 of the other player's cups wins. The host can tap **REMATCH**, and the loser throws first.
- Playing alone? The host can add a bot.

## Kopi Tumpah (2–6 players)

Pick **KOPI TUMPAH** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA.

- Everyone takes turns pouring kopi into the same glass. **Press and hold TUANG** (or the space bar) to pour; the longer you hold, the faster it pours. A few drops still fall after you let go.
- Any pour counts, even a single drip. Every cup starts empty. You have 10 seconds, or the kettle drips a little for you.
- The kopi can bulge above the rim, but the real spill point is secret and changes every cup.
- Whoever makes it **tumpah** is out and belanja kopi. A new cup starts, and the last player left wins.
- When the game ends, the host can tap **MAIN LAGI** to play again, and anyone can tap **MAIN MENU** to go back to the first screen.

## Ingat Order (1–6 players)

Pick **INGAT ORDER** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA. You can also play alone.

- You are the waiter. Players take turns, one table each, 5 tables per player.
- Customers say their orders one at a time in speech bubbles, then the bubbles disappear.
- Key the whole order into the tablet from memory: drinks are PANAS (hot) or AIS, plus food. Use − and + for quantities. You have 30 seconds, then tap HANTAR ORDER.
- Tables get harder: 1 customer at table 1, up to 4 at tables 4–5. From table 3 someone may shout "tambah satu...", and at table 5 someone also changes (tukar) their order.
- Scoring: +10 per right item, −5 per wrong or extra item, +20 for a perfect table. The final ranking shows each player's points and perfect tables.

## Cari Telur (1–6 players)

Pick **CARI TELUR** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA. You can also play alone.

- Watch the egg go under one of the pink bowls, then follow it while the bowls shuffle.
- Everyone picks at the same time by tapping a bowl (10 seconds). Nobody sees the other picks until the reveal.
- A right pick scores 100 points, plus up to 50 for picking fast. Nobody is knocked out.
- 10 levels: 3 bowls at first, then 4, 5 and finally 6 bowls, faster each level. From level 6 some bowls jump over others and some swaps are fakes.
- The next level starts by itself after the reveal; the host can tap NEXT to skip ahead.

## Tarik Teh (1–6 players)

Pick **TARIK TEH** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA. You can also play alone.

- Players take turns, one pull each, for 3 rounds.
- Press and hold on the mugs to pour. While you hold, the top mug keeps rising: higher means more buih (froth) per second, but the stream swings more.
- Slide left and right to keep the bottom mug under the stream. On a computer you can also hold Space and use the arrow keys.
- Teh that misses fills the TUMPAH bar. If it fills up, that pull scores 0. Let go any time to keep your buih.
- Pour the whole mug with almost no spill for a SEMPURNA bonus of 25. The most buih after 3 rounds wins.

## Kira Duit (1–6 players)

Pick **KIRA DUIT** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA. You can also play alone.

- A customer comes to the BAYAR SINI counter. The receipt shows what they ate, the total and what they paid with.
- Everyone works out the baki (change) at the same time. Tap notes and coins to put them in your tray, then press BAGI BAKI. One try per customer.
- A right baki scores 100 for the fastest, then 75, 60, 50 and so on. Using the fewest pieces adds 10. A wrong baki or no answer scores 0.
- 10 customers, with more items each time. Later customers sometimes pay with extra coins so the baki comes out neat.

## Lalat Hunt (1–6 players)

Pick **LALAT HUNT** on the first screen before tapping BUKA MEJA. You can also play alone.

- Flies buzz around a kopitiam table seen from above and land for a moment. Everyone plays at the same time.
- Tap a fly, flying or landed, to swat it with your selipar. The first player to hit it gets the points. Landed flies are easier to hit.
- Black lalat +10, fat green langau +25, rare golden lalat +50.
- Missing and hitting a plate or cup is −15. Swatting empty table makes you wait a moment.
- 3 rounds of 30 seconds. Round 2 has more flies and new plates; round 3 is a swarm.

## Uncle Jack 21 (secret game)

Uncle Jack 21 is hidden from the first screen. Tap the **KOPITIAM 21** signboard 9 times quickly to unlock it.

### House rules

- Everyone starts with **1000 chips**. These are play chips only, with no real money.
- Blackjack pays 3:2. Insurance pays 2:1. The Tauke stands on 17. You can double on any two cards, and split up to 4 hands.
- **The Tauke's chips are the bank.** Players win from the Tauke and lose to the Tauke.
- **No credit.** A player who runs out of chips sits out. If the Tauke goes broke, they pay out what they can and a new Tauke is picked at random.
- The game ends when only one person still has chips. The host can then start a **NEW GAME**.

## Good to know

- **The host's browser runs the table.** The host should keep that tab open and the screen on. The game asks the phone to stay awake, but if the host closes the tab, the table closes for everyone.
- If a player refreshes the page or loses signal, they rejoin the same seat automatically.
- Messages go through a free public relay (MQTT over secure WebSocket: EMQX, with HiveMQ as backup). It works on any Wi-Fi or mobile data, and nobody needs an account. You can change the relay list in `js/config.js`.

## Testing alone

Open `index.html?offline&autohost&bots=4&autostart` for Uncle Jack 21 against 4 bots, or `index.html?offline&autohost&bots=1&autostart&mode=pong` for Telur Pong against a bot, or `index.html?offline&autohost&bots=3&mode=kopi` for Kopi Tumpah against 3 bots. No network is used.
